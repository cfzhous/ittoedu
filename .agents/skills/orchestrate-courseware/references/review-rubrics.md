# Human review rubrics

These rubrics guide a named human; they never auto-approve a scope.

## Courseware contract

Confirm that:

- audience, prerequisites, usage context, duration, and authority order are explicit;
- every observable objective has meaningful evidence, not mere viewing or clicking;
- every `EVD-*` has an intentional `RESP-*` collection channel; oral, paper, teacher-observed, and discussion evidence is not silently converted into page input;
- exact content is complete, correct, appropriately difficult, sourced or independently reviewed, and recoverable without chat;
- answers, explanations, alternatives, invalid boundaries, errors, feedback, reveal order, and capacity are closed;
- the response-capacity total includes reading/observation, transitions, conservative type floors, retry, and teacher discussion; every below-floor override is an answered material decision;
- assessment authority matches the task: finite/normalized automation names a real capability and tolerance matrix, while open semantic work remains human and never hard-locks navigation;
- each `AUTH-*` promises a maintenance access level appropriate to the content, layout, and style work teachers will actually need;
- the product profile explicitly states single-device, teacher-display, offline, and multi-user-aggregation requirements; unsupported required capabilities have an acceptable fallback or answered decision;
- every teaching stage contributes to an objective, misconception repair, or transfer;
- assumptions and constraints do not silently override user-supplied material.

Reject titles/activity labels used in place of exact content, generic quizzes without evidence value, or time justified only by slide/interaction count.

## Presentation script

Confirm that:

- every scene references approved objectives, evidence, and exact content;
- the initial and pre-action view contains everything needed for the first action;
- student/teacher control, information release, hidden states, success, error, retry, reveal, and recovery are explicit;
- action and response form a visible teaching cause, not decorative interaction;
- every required student/teacher behavior has an `ACT-*` with a visible target, real response producer where applicable, and observable pre-action/error/retry/reveal/stable results;
- every scene has an `ESC-*` that remains available from blank, error, and incomplete states without first requiring a correct machine judgment; continue-incomplete explicitly confirms intent;
- stable states, transitions, replay/reopen behavior, and teacher checkpoints are reachable and unambiguous;
- interaction frames and PDF/PPTX static frames remain meaningful;
- the final synthesis is earned from earlier evidence.

Read it as a cold-start implementer. Reject it if the Builder must invent visible content, collection channel, evaluator authority, capacity, editability, action target, escape behavior, feedback, or outcomes.

## High-risk visual direction

Confirm that:

- the primary subject representation, hierarchy, composition, interaction target, and typography are clear;
- the representative prototype covers the expensive or uncertain initial, action, error, and result states;
- motion communicates instructional causality and supports reduced motion;
- assets exist or have an approved source/generation/license route;
- HTML and static-delivery differences are explicit and acceptable;
- the direction does not impose repetitive slide chrome or card grids that weaken the experience.

## Readiness and outcome separation

`implementation-ready` means the approved experience contract is complete enough for the V8 Builder. It does not approve a technical carrier, export, visual result, or final product. Automated checks may later establish at most `engineering candidate`; only explicit human outcome review may establish `accepted`.
